# Page design

Before you add or change anything a Wings page shows (a plugin tab, view, section, block or app page), load the `wings-page-design` skill and follow it. Don't call a page change done until you've rendered it with `pnpm --dir plugins shot <out-dir> --<plugin>` (the `shot_plugins` example) and looked at the screenshots at 1000 and 640 points.
